# Changelog

The module got its own repository at version 1.2.1; the entries before it are summaries.

## 1.2.1

- A player's particles (Kostis's Particle Effects) are no longer taken for intruders.

## 1.2.0

- Combat pauses patrols, patrols remember whether they are running, closed doors re-route them, and scenes with
  levels are supported.
